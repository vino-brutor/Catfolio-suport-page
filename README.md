# Catfolio / Gatoteca — support page

Static support and privacy page for the Catfolio/Gatoteca app, with Portuguese and English content.

## How to open

Click [`Here`](https://vino-brutor.github.io/Catfolio-suport-page/) to acess the web page.

## What the page includes

- PT/EN language switcher.
- Frequently asked questions about entries, camera access, and coat identification.
- Privacy policy explaining that the app does not collect data and has no accounts, ads, or tracking.
- Contact email.
- Visual style inspired by the app's kraft/scrapbook aesthetic.
